Selected work

Rainfall Atlas

Interactive map for the Mumbai Climate Desk, 2025

A ward-by-ward map of 30 years of monsoon rainfall. Readers drag a timeline and watch flood-prone areas change. Built with D3, MapLibre, and a small Node data pipeline. It was embedded in 14 regional news sites.

Commute Lines

Transit dashboard for a city planning team, 2024

A live dashboard that compares train, bus, and ferry delays across routes. Planners filter by hour and see the worst stretches first. Built with TypeScript, Observable Plot, and Postgres. It replaced a 40-tab spreadsheet.

Small Numbers

Personal project: a visual diary, ongoing

Every week I chart one tiny measurement from my life, such as cups of chai, steps to the station, or unread emails. Built with plain HTML, SVG, and a lot of curiosity.
